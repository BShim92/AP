Guide rule & source

The guide flags at least three occupied reels when two or more are among reels 1–3. This covers all three left reels, two left plus one right, and any four or five reels.

This is the base game coin check for Golden Egypt. An already active wild feature needs a separate check. The rule is the guide author’s estimate.

Guide rules & source
  • Core rule for frames on reels 1–3: 2 after spin 1, 3 after spin 2, or 4 after spins 3–9.
  • Wider rule: 2 on reels 1–3 after spins 1–4. After spins 5–9, look for 2 on reels 1–3 plus one more anywhere on reels 1–4.
  • Blank counts equal zero. The guide treats the wider rule as a rough estimate and flags gaps as risky.
