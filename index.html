Wild in positions 2–5
Count from the left

A stored wild in any of those spots qualifies. Each spin moves it one spot left. A wild already in the far-left spot disappears on the next spin.

  • Minor, Maxi, Major or Grand One filled spot qualifies on spins 1 through 9.
  • Mini One filled spot qualifies with four or fewer spins left. Look for 6–9 out of 10 on the display. Earlier, also look for a strong wild layout.
  • 10 out of 10 The next spin starts a fresh cycle.
What counts as a strong wild layout?

Look for existing wild spots that already form multiple three-of-a-kind ways or several four-of-a-kind ways or better. Read the reels from left to right and give less weight to layouts with a gap of two empty reels.

Reading the bet level

Use the multiplier on the bet button. For example, if the 1× button is 50 credits, the 150-credit button is 3×. A bet button can start a spin when the machine has enough credits.
