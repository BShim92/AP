Check rules
  • If a prize shows 3×, enter 3. A blank counts as 1×. Skip the top-left square.
  • The small numbers in the corners are score weights. Starting at 13.40, the check adds each entered multiplier minus 1, times that square’s weight.
  • Below 17 shows Below AP threshold. From 17 to under 17.5, the result is Play for fun/points.
  • From 17.5 through 20, the result is Frankenstein. Above 20, it’s Frankenstein Returns.
Enter bet size

Check rules
  • Use the same units for the bet and coin values. For example, a 1.60 coin on a 0.80 bet counts as 2 bets. Empty cells count as zero.
  • Only the bottom two rows count. A wheel appears as a dot and adds 38 bets when it’s in one of those rows. Use + Row to record a row above them.
  • Below 50 bets shows Below AP threshold. From 50 to under 60, the result is Near AP threshold. At 60 or more, it’s Meets AP threshold.
  • At 50 to under 60 bets, review the third and fourth rows for strong values. Use Red ball to review for the separate 100× or 200× placement checks. Enter its displayed coin value in the grid if it’s in a counted row.

Enter reel counts
Spin 1

Check rules
  • Number the reels from left to right. If the machine shows 8 out of 10, choose 8.
  • Spin 1: At least one gold symbol on reels 1–4.
  • Spin 2: At least one on reels 1–3, two on reel 4, or one on reel 4 plus two on reel 5.
  • Spin 4: At least three total, or a stack on any reel from 1–4. A stack is two symbols on the same reel.
  • Spin 6: At least four total, or at least three on reels 1–3 combined.
  • Spin 8: Three or more on reels 1–3, or one of these patterns:
    • A stack on reels 1–3 plus a symbol on reel 4 or a stack on reel 5.
    • Two on reels 1–3 plus a stack on reel 4, or plus one on reel 4 and a stack on reel 5.
    • One on reels 1–3 plus stacks on both reels 4 and 5.
  • The app estimates spins 3, 5, and 7 using the next listed rule. At spin 9, the check shows Reevaluate.
  • Good through uses the current counts, including those estimates. Update the counts and check again when the symbols change.

Enter spin and frames

For standard four-row versions with guaranteed wild conversion.

Guide rules
  • If the machine shows 8 out of 10, choose 8. Count reels from left to right and skip reel 5.
  • Guide rule: At spins 1–4, look for at least 2 frames on reels 1–3 combined. At spins 5–9, look for those 2 plus one more on any of reels 1–4.
  • Optional tighter check: On reels 1–3 combined, look for at least 2 frames at spin 1, 3 at spin 2, or 4 at spins 3–9.
  • The five-row, exploding-scarab, jackpot, and non-guaranteed-wild versions use different rules.
  • One blank count is treated as zero. If both counts are zero, enter 0 in either field.

Guide rule

Count each group separately. If you see one group of 5 and another of 3, enter 5.

Six or more touching empty frames meets the guide cutoff. At five, review the layout for a second group of five or a lot of nearby empty frames that could connect.
